Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Diani Splash Kids.

Our water park is open daily from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The Splash Kitchen & Bar restaurant operates from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM, serving lunch, dinner, snacks, and drinks throughout the day.

Diani Splash Kids is designed primarily for children aged 3 to 15 years. We have dedicated toddler areas with shallow pools and mini slides for ages 3–5, as well as more thrilling water slides and activities for older children. Adults are welcome to join in the fun too!

We offer individual activity pricing so you only pay for what you enjoy. Sliding area (pool side) is KES 1,000, Toddlers KES 800, Indoor Games KES 500, Robots KES 300, Hover Board / Mini Cars / Floaters KES 200 each, and Bicycles KES 100 for 20 minutes. See our full pricing section above for details.

Absolutely! Birthday parties are one of our specialties. We offer a stress-free party experience that includes themed decorations, child-friendly catering (pizza, snacks, ice cream), supervised games and entertainment, and special surprises for the birthday child. Call +254 114 150 622 to check availability and customize your package.

We are located at 80401 Diani Beach Road, Galu Beach – Salama Villas No. 1, near Neptune Hotels in Diani, Kenya. We are conveniently situated along the main Diani Beach road, easily accessible for both local families and tourists.

Yes! The Splash Kitchen & Bar is open daily from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM. We serve bistro fare, fast food, healthy options, snacks, and a full range of drinks. Our specialty is brick-oven pizza, and we offer free delivery within a 5km radius. We also have a dedicated kids menu with mini burgers, sandwiches, and child-friendly meals.

Safety is our top priority. Our facility accommodates approximately 50–65 children simultaneously in a controlled environment. All water attractions are supervised by trained staff and lifeguards. We maintain strict safety protocols, with age-appropriate equipment and dedicated toddler zones separated from deeper water areas.

We offer a wide range of attractions including water slides (wave slides, tube slides, spiral slides), splash pads and paddling pools, six water towers with interactive features, a bouncy castle, mini-cars (ages 3–5), billiards, robots and hover boards, floaters and bicycles. Quad bike tours are also available by contacting us directly.
